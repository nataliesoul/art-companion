Artist Companion

Composition guides and thoughtful feedback for physical art.

1. Artwork photo

Choose a photo of one flat rectangular artwork. Your original stays untouched; guides draw on top only.

2. Mark the four corners

Add a photo first, then tap its four corners in any order.

AI overview

One automatic read of the whole artwork: composition, values, color, balance, marks on the image, and what to try next. It runs once your corners are set, if you allow it, and sends the rectified artwork with your palette, medium and intent to the AI service.

Analyze automatically?

Each time your corners are set, two small images of the marked artwork plus your palette, medium and intent go to the AI service. You can change this any time below.

AI not configured

              9. Albers color studies

              Studies after Josef Albers, Interaction of Color (1963). Descriptions below are original. Screens vary, so treat these as studies, not proofs.

              Same color, different readings

              Both centers are pixel-identical #8a8a8a; the grounds change how each reads. A color is never seen alone.

              Afterimage

              Stare at a saturated color for about 20 seconds, then look at a neutral gray: a ghost of the complementary color appears. The interactive study below times this for you.

              Vibration

              High-chroma neighbors of similar lightness shimmer at their boundary. Try it with your own colors below.
